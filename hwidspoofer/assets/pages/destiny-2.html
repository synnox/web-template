FAQ

What should Destiny 2 players know about HWID bans?

Destiny 2 HWID ban FAQs explain BattlEye enforcement and the supported TraceX workflow.

Does Destiny 2 actually issue HWID bans, or only account bans?

Bungie explicitly distinguishes account bans from device bans. Its policy says a device-banned player loses access to the named Destiny activities on that hardware and that no account can participate there, regardless of the account's own ban status. Device bans may follow multiple confirmed offenses or a first offense Bungie considers egregious. The ban message, not a generic disconnect code, identifies which level applies.

Does Error Code: WEASEL mean I have a Destiny 2 HWID ban?

No. Bungie's Weasel help page calls it an all-encompassing error that usually indicates a general network problem. A player who is online when a ban or restriction is applied is also disconnected with Weasel, but Bungie says that code is the result of the enforcement, not its cause. Look for the separate message that explicitly references an account or device ban before treating a Weasel disconnect as hardware enforcement.

What hardware identifiers can BattlEye see in Destiny 2?

BattlEye publicly confirms hardware-device information and identifiers such as serial numbers, but it does not publish a Destiny-specific field list. Relevant Windows surfaces include the SMBIOS Type 2 baseboard serial, system UUID, disk firmware and volume serials, physical NIC MAC addresses, and MachineGuid under HKLM\SOFTWARE\Microsoft\Cryptography. TPM 2.0 endorsement keys and Secure Boot state are distinct platform surfaces; their use as Destiny ban keys is not publicly confirmed.

Will formatting Windows or reinstalling Destiny 2 clear a device ban?

No reinstall removes Bungie's server-side account or device sanction. Reinstalling Destiny 2 can fix missing BattlEye files or a damaged service, and a clean Windows install can regenerate MachineGuid plus some volume metadata. It does not replace the SMBIOS baseboard serial, motherboard UUID, controller-reported disk serials, physical NIC identity, or TPM-rooted material. A format changes only part of the machine profile.

Will a new Bungie or Steam account work on a Destiny 2 device-banned PC?

Not for the Destiny activities named in the device-ban message. Bungie's policy says no account can participate in those activities on a banned device, regardless of the individual account's status. A new login changes the Bungie or platform identity but does not rescind the device record or recover the old account's Guardians and entitlements. If you only saw Weasel, PLUM, or PLUOT, diagnose that code before assuming the PC is device-banned.

Are PLUM and PLUOT the same as a Destiny 2 hardware ban?

No. Bungie's PLUM page says BattlEye detected a problem with the computer that may violate the Terms of Service; that warning is not the literal device-ban message. PLUOT is a launch error shown when BattlEye is denied, uninstalled, or Destiny 2 is started outside Steam, Epic Games Store, or Microsoft Store. Fixing an installation problem can clear those errors. A confirmed device ban separately says the hardware is banned.

Can a Destiny 2 BattlEye ban carry over to PUBG, DayZ, or Siege?

Do not treat it as an automatic roster-wide ban. BattlEye can collect the same broad hardware-information category in every protected integration, so reusing the old values creates cross-game recognition exposure. The publisher and game integration still administer the enforcement record. A Destiny 2 device ban does not by itself prove that a PUBG, DayZ, or Rainbow Six Siege account is banned; the shared collection mechanism is the risk, not a universal ban list.

Can I appeal a Destiny 2 device ban, and who reviews it?

Use Bungie's Ban/Restriction Appeal Contact Form for a Destiny account or device action. Bungie asks for evidence, says there is no guarantee a ban will be overturned, and warns that not every submission receives a reply. Requests for the ban reason or restriction length are disregarded. Do not spam duplicate forms. A BattlEye Global Ban #ID belongs through BattlEye's appeal route, but the message must actually show that global-ban ID.

Will Destiny 2 work on Steam Deck through SteamOS or Proton?

Bungie says Destiny 2 is unsupported on Steam Deck unless Windows is installed and running the game. Launching through SteamOS returns the player to the library, and Bungie's policy says trying to bypass the SteamOS or Proton incompatibility results in a game ban. That is a publisher rule, not proof that every normal BattlEye startup error is enforcement. Use a supported Windows installation and a supported store launcher.
