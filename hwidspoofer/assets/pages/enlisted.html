FAQ

What should Enlisted players know about HWID bans?

Enlisted HWID ban FAQs explain BattlEye enforcement and the supported TraceX workflow.

Does Enlisted really issue HWID bans, or only account bans?

Enlisted officially uses BattlEye, whose privacy policy says it may process hardware-device information and identifiers such as serial numbers. Gaijin also publishes permanent OUTLISTED account actions after replay review. Neither source says every Enlisted ban includes a device block or publishes the game's matching threshold. A repeated sanction on new credentials from one unchanged PC can be consistent with hardware recognition, but the exact notice and account status still matter.

How can I tell an Enlisted HWID ban from a BattlEye error?

Read the wording before changing anything. BattlEye identifies a global action with "Global Ban #ID" when the game displays one; a permanent Gaijin notice is also enforcement. "Failed to initialize BattlEye Service," driver-load errors, blocked-file notices, and a request to restart are technical failures or kicks, not proof of an HWID ban. Repair the BattlEye installation path for a service error. Use the ban route only when the message or account record shows a sanction.

What hardware identifiers can BattlEye see in Enlisted?

BattlEye publicly confirms the broad category of hardware-device information and identifiers such as serial numbers, not Enlisted's private field list. Relevant Windows surfaces include the SMBIOS Type 2 baseboard serial, SMBIOS system or motherboard UUID, disk firmware and volume serials, physical NIC MAC addresses, and MachineGuid under HKLM\SOFTWARE\Microsoft\Cryptography. TPM 2.0 endorsement-key material and Secure Boot state are separate surfaces; neither is confirmed as an Enlisted ban key.

Will reinstalling Enlisted or formatting Windows clear the ban?

Reinstalling Enlisted can repair damaged game files or a broken BattlEye service, but it cannot remove a server-side Gaijin or BattlEye sanction. Formatting Windows may regenerate MachineGuid and filesystem volume metadata. It does not normally replace the SMBIOS board serial, system UUID, drive firmware serial, physical NIC identity, or TPM-rooted material. A clean install is useful troubleshooting for service faults; it is not a complete machine-identity reset.

Will a new Gaijin or Steam account work after an Enlisted hardware ban?

New credentials change the account identifier, not the hardware information BattlEye says it may process. If Gaijin's action is account-only, the device may not be the deciding signal; if the machine profile is recognized, the same board, disk, Windows, and network values remain available. No official Enlisted policy promises that a replacement account will work, and creating accounts to evade a confirmed sanction can compound the enforcement rather than recover the original progress.

Does an Enlisted ban automatically affect War Thunder or other BattlEye games?

No automatic roster-wide rule is supportable. Enlisted and War Thunder share a Gaijin account ecosystem and now both use BattlEye, so unchanged hardware surfaces can be encountered again inside the same publisher stack. PUBG, DayZ, Siege, and other BattlEye titles can also see comparable device categories. Publishers still control title-level sanctions; cross-game fingerprint exposure is real, but an Enlisted ban is not proof that every other BattlEye game has banned you.

Does Enlisted still use Easy Anti-Cheat in 2026?

No. Darkflow and Gaijin announced Enlisted's transition to BattlEye on September 16, 2024, with the replacement applied automatically during a client launch. Current Gaijin troubleshooting also points to the Enlisted BattlEye folder under the Steam installation. Pages that still describe Enlisted as an Easy Anti-Cheat title are using pre-transition information. That distinction matters because EAC troubleshooting, appeal wording, and service paths are not the right references for the current client.

What is OUTLISTED, and is it the same as an automatic BattlEye ban?

OUTLISTED is Gaijin's public series for permanent Enlisted actions confirmed after player complaints and replay review by game masters. Gaijin says reports can start through the in-battle Complain option or the Replays section of its site. That human-reviewed route sits beside the external BattlEye layer and Enlisted's built-in client protection; it is not evidence that every name on an OUTLISTED sheet received a separate HWID ban. How do I appeal an Enlisted BattlEye or Gaijin ban?

Use the authority shown by the notice. For a BattlEye global action, BattlEye asks for the exact "Global Ban #ID" and the game name; if Enlisted shows no ID, provide the game-account identifier. For a Gaijin account action or OUTLISTED review, use Gaijin Support and describe the notice accurately. BattlEye says duplicate submissions do not accelerate review and confirmed global bans are permanent, so one complete appeal is more useful than repeated tickets or service troubleshooting sent to the wrong team.
