What should Throne and Liberty players know about HWID bans?

Throne and Liberty HWID ban FAQs explain EasyAntiCheat enforcement and the supported TraceX workflow.

Does an Easy Anti-Cheat notice mean a Throne and Liberty HWID ban?

An Easy Anti-Cheat notice confirms an account penalty categorized as an EAC detection. It does not reveal whether Amazon also stored or enforced a hardware match. Check the penalty portal, preserve the exact reason, and use the single appeal if the action is wrong. A recurring restriction on another clean account from the same PC may be consistent with device correlation, but the message alone is not a public HWID verdict.

Does Throne and Liberty actually issue HWID bans?

Amazon's public Throne and Liberty support pages document cheating penalties, known-cheat detections, and permanent account outcomes, but they do not publish a hardware-ban schema or duration. EAC has the technical surface to build a composite device profile, and player reports describe same-PC correlation, yet those reports cannot prove Amazon's threshold. Treat hardware enforcement as possible without presenting a private matching formula as confirmed fact.

Which identifiers can be part of a Throne and Liberty EAC hardware profile?

Relevant Windows surfaces include the SMBIOS Type 2 baseboard serial, motherboard UUID, physical-disk firmware serials queried through IOCTL_STORAGE_QUERY_PROPERTY, filesystem volume serials, physical NIC MAC addresses, and MachineGuid under HKLM\SOFTWARE\Microsoft\Cryptography. TPM 2.0 endorsement-key material and Secure Boot can add identity or trust context. Amazon and Epic do not confirm Throne and Liberty's exact selection or weighting.

Will a new Steam account work after a Throne and Liberty hardware ban?

A new Steam login changes the account identity, not the board, disk, Windows, or network values exposed by the PC. If Amazon's restriction is device-linked, enough of the old composite profile can remain for correlation. An alternate account is also a poor diagnostic because another sanction does not reveal which signal caused it. Appeal the original penalty first; TraceX cannot recover that sanctioned Steam account.

Will reinstalling Throne and Liberty, Steam, or Windows clear a TL HWID ban?

Reinstalling the game or Steam replaces application files and can repair an EAC startup problem; it does not change the machine's firmware or controller identities. Reinstalling Windows may regenerate MachineGuid and formatting can change a volume serial, while the SMBIOS Type 2 serial, motherboard UUID, physical-disk firmware serials, factory NIC MAC addresses, and TPM-rooted material remain. A clean install is not a complete hardware-profile reset.

Is "Kicked Reason: Easy-Anti Cheat(47)" a Throne and Liberty hardware ban?

No conclusion follows from Code 47 alone. Players have reported that disconnect during ordinary sessions and service incidents, so it should be treated as an EAC kick until the penalty portal shows an actual sanction. Restart and file-integrity checks may resolve a service problem, but do not repeatedly test new accounts. If the portal lists a cheating penalty, diagnose and appeal that account action separately from the kick.

Can I appeal a Throne and Liberty EAC or permanent ban?

Yes. Throne and Liberty support says each penalty can be appealed once. Review the Code of Conduct and Penalty Categories first, then submit the exact penalty text, why it is incorrect, relevant account-security context, and any useful evidence in that one appeal. Amazon does not promise to disclose detection details. A hardware rewrite cannot overturn the server-side decision or restore access to the sanctioned Steam account.

Will a Throne and Liberty EAC ban automatically ban Fortnite or Rust?

No universal EAC roster-wide ban is documented. Multiple games can run the same EAC collection layer and encounter the same classes of motherboard, storage, Windows, network, and security-state values, so an already flagged profile can create cross-title re-identification risk. Publishers still administer their own sanctions. A Throne and Liberty penalty therefore does not automatically become a Fortnite or Rust ban.

Can a used motherboard or PC carry a Throne and Liberty hardware match?

A used PC presents the previous owner's SMBIOS board serial, motherboard UUID, installed-drive firmware serials, and factory network identities, so inherited device correlation is technically possible. Amazon does not publish Throne and Liberty's match threshold, which means a first-launch failure on second-hand hardware is not proof of an inherited ban. Check the exact EAC message, Steam penalty record, service status, and software state first.
