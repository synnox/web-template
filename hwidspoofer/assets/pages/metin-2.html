FAQ

What should Metin 2 players know about HWID bans?

Metin 2 HWID ban FAQs explain HackShield enforcement and the supported TraceX workflow.

Does Metin 2 use GameGuard or HackShield?

Gameforge's own Metin2 UK team said on July 27, 2015 that GameGuard was replacing HackShield. Its April 24, 2020 ban-wave FAQ then discussed a separate Cheat Blocker process named `cb.exe`. That makes "HackShield has remained the live anti-cheat since 2010" an outdated claim. Gameforge does not publish a 2026 architecture page naming every active client component, so the defensible answer is the documented transition, not a guessed current stack.

Does "The game is currently unavailable to you" mean an HWID ban?

Not by itself. The wording is a real Gameforge Client restriction, but Gameforge has not published it as a dedicated Metin 2 hardware-ban code. Reports on its forum include cases that cleared after waiting or Support review, which means an account or launcher false positive can look similar. Save the exact message, time, affected Gameforge account, and Support response. Do not cycle valuable shop accounts through the PC to manufacture a diagnosis.

What hardware identifiers can identify a Metin 2 PC?

Relevant Windows surfaces include the SMBIOS Type 2 baseboard serial, SMBIOS Type 1 system UUID, physical disk serials returned through storage IOCTLs, separate volume serials, physical NIC MAC addresses, and `MachineGuid` under `HKLM\SOFTWARE\Microsoft\Cryptography`. TPM 2.0 has an endorsement key, while Secure Boot exposes platform state. These surfaces are real; Gameforge does not publish which ones Metin2 stores or how it weights them.

Will making a new Gameforge account let me play after a Metin 2 ban?

A new login answers only the account side, and testing it can put another account at risk. Gameforge's 2020 FAQ confirms that Cheat Blocker bans can involve accounts it links to a botting account, but it does not publish that linkage formula or say every new account on one PC is automatically banned. If Support confirms only the original account is sanctioned, a hardware rewrite cannot restore it. If the launcher restriction is unresolved, appeal before testing more accounts.

Will reinstalling Metin 2 or Windows remove an HWID ban?

Reinstalling Metin2 replaces client files and can repair a broken patch, so it is useful for a client fault. It does not change the SMBIOS board serial, system UUID, drive-firmware serials, or physical NIC identity. Formatting Windows may regenerate `MachineGuid` and volume metadata, but durable firmware and TPM-rooted surfaces normally remain. Because Gameforge does not disclose its matching recipe, neither action is proof that a confirmed device restriction has been cleared.

Can a VPN bypass a Metin 2 hardware ban?

No. A VPN changes the public IP address used to reach Gameforge, not the SMBIOS baseboard serial, system UUID, physical disk serial, volume serial, NIC hardware, `MachineGuid`, TPM endorsement key, or Secure Boot state. Gameforge's April 2020 FAQ also says players were not banned merely for using a VPN during those Cheat Blocker waves. A VPN can change routing; it cannot turn the same Windows PC into a different hardware profile.

How does Metin 2 account linkage actually work?

Gameforge's April 2020 FAQ says level-one and shop accounts were sometimes banned because they were linked to another account that was botting. It also says cases were reviewed individually and accounts on separate Gameforge accounts were not automatically banned merely because a player later cheated elsewhere. Gameforge withheld the linkage signals to protect detection. Claims that one guild invite or marketplace trade always propagates a ban go beyond the published record.

Can I appeal a Metin 2 ban or launcher restriction?

Yes. Gameforge directs in-game ban disputes to its Metin2 Support system. Include the exact Client wording, when it started, which game account is affected, and whether other games in the same Gameforge account still open. Do not claim compromise, shared access, or a false positive unless it is true. Gameforge's Product Manager said deliberate external-tool cases with hard evidence are upheld, and the company does not disclose detailed telemetry that would weaken detection.

Does a Metin 2 ban carry over to other GameGuard games?

There is no published automatic cross-publisher ban list. GameGuard can encounter the same stable Windows identity surfaces in multiple protected titles, so cross-game fingerprint exposure is technically possible.
