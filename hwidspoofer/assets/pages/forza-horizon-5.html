What should Forza Horizon 5 players know about HWID bans?

Forza Horizon 5 HWID ban FAQs explain Arbiter Anti-Cheat enforcement and the supported TraceX workflow.

Does Forza Horizon 5 use Easy Anti-Cheat or BattlEye for HWID bans?

Do not trust a product page that labels FH5 as EAC by default. Forza's public support material describes its enforcement rules but does not identify Easy Anti-Cheat, BattlEye, or Vanguard as FH5's engine, and it does not publish the name or fingerprint schema of the PC detection component. The useful evidence is the sanction shown in Message Center or Forza Ban Info, not a third-party engine logo copied from another game.

How do I know whether my FH5 ban is a feature ban or a hardware ban?

The literal message matters. "Your Gamertag is temporarily banned from this feature" identifies a limited action affecting multiplayer, Rivals, or the Auction House, and the Message Center supplies its duration. A hardware ban becomes more plausible when different Microsoft accounts are blocked only after they use the same PC, matching Forza's policy that associated accounts on a banned device can be blocked. One crash or online error is not proof of an HWID ban.

Which identifiers can matter in a Forza Horizon 5 device ban?

Forza does not publish an FH5 field list or matching formula. Relevant Windows surfaces include the SMBIOS Type 2 baseboard serial, motherboard UUID, physical disk serials exposed through storage IOCTLs, filesystem volume serials, physical NIC MAC addresses, and MachineGuid under HKLM\SOFTWARE\Microsoft\Cryptography. TPM 2.0 endorsement-key material and Secure Boot state are separate hardware-rooted signals, but no first-party Forza source confirms either as an FH5 ban key.

Will reinstalling Windows or replacing my SSD remove an FH5 hardware ban?

Not reliably. A Windows reinstall can replace MachineGuid and the system volume serial, while a new SSD changes one physical drive serial. Neither action rewrites the SMBIOS baseboard serial, system UUID, other connected drive serials, or the factory identity of a physical network adapter. Because Forza does not disclose which fields it uses or how they are weighted, claims that a format or single drive swap always clears an FH5 device record are guesses.

Can a new account bypass a Forza Horizon 5 device ban?

Changing the storefront or login changes the account layer, not the PC. Steam and Game Pass for PC still require the Microsoft account and Xbox services used by FH5 online features. Forza's Code of Conduct says a hardware ban can block all associated accounts on that device and that alternate accounts used to circumvent enforcement can also be suspended. A new entitlement does not erase the old account sanction or prove that the machine association is gone.

Will an FH5 hardware ban carry over to another Forza game?

Cross-title action is possible, but do not turn it into an automatic rule. Forza's policy says permanent banning can terminate accounts from its games and can block associated accounts on a device; its ban guidance also warns that later behavior in other Forza titles can bring stronger enforcement. It does not say every FH5 feature suspension instantly bans every Forza release. The real blast radius is the shared Forza account and device association, not a universal EAC ban list.

Why is only the FH5 Auction House or Rivals unavailable?

Forza can suspend one feature without issuing a full account or hardware ban. Its Code of Conduct specifically names matchmaking and the Auction House as feature-level targets, while the support notice says "Your Gamertag is temporarily banned from this feature." Rivals entries can also be removed through server-side leaderboard cleanup. Check the Message Center and Forza Ban Info before treating a greyed-out tile as evidence that the entire PC has been banned.

Can a VPN bypass a Forza Horizon 5 hardware ban?

No. A VPN changes the public IP address presented to an online service, but it does not rewrite an SMBIOS board serial, motherboard UUID, physical disk or volume serial, MachineGuid, TPM-rooted material, Secure Boot state, or the MAC address of the physical NIC. It can add a virtual network adapter and make diagnosis noisier without changing the persistent PC profile. Use the exact Forza sanction record, not a changed IP, to identify the ban layer.

How long does an FH5 hardware ban last, and can I appeal it?

Forza says suspensions can range from 24 hours to one month to permanent, depending on severity and repeat behavior; a hardware ban is part of its permanent-enforcement policy.
