What does a game ban wave mean?

A cluster of reports is a signal, not a verdict. Detection, review, and enforcement are separate stages, and the pipeline behind a hardware ban explains why a sweep can land days after the behaviour that triggered it. Enforcement is also administered per publisher, so a spike in one title says nothing definitive about another game running the same engine.

To see which engine sits behind a given title, open the supported games list or compare the engines themselves in the anti-cheat directory. If your account was caught in the sweep, the first steps after a ban lands covers what to check before changing anything on the machine.
