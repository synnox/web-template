FAQ

What should Overwatch 2 players know about HWID bans?

Overwatch 2 HWID ban FAQs explain Blizzard Anti-Cheat enforcement and the supported TraceX workflow.

Does Overwatch 2 actually issue HWID bans, or only account bans?

Blizzard publicly confirms permanent Overwatch 2 account bans, but its Defense Matrix posts do not publish an HWID-ban policy or the fields in a device fingerprint. Treat "Account Banned" as proof of account enforcement. A hardware link becomes a reasonable diagnosis only when account, network, and software causes have been separated and repeat actions consistently follow one PC. That is evidence, not disclosure of Blizzard's formula.

Why was a new Battle.net or Steam account banned on the same PC?

Steam does not create a separate Overwatch identity; it still links to Battle.net. A repeat action can therefore come from the linked account, prohibited software, behavior, or a correlated device profile. One fast ban is not enough to identify which signal fired. Do not keep feeding new accounts into the same setup. Remove prohibited software, secure the Battle.net account, preserve the emails, and use Blizzard's appeal process before assuming an HWID match.

Which PC identifiers can survive an Overwatch 2 reinstall?

Stable Windows-visible surfaces include the SMBIOS Type 2 baseboard serial, motherboard UUID, disk firmware and volume serials exposed through storage IOCTLs, network-adapter MAC addresses, and MachineGuid under HKLM\SOFTWARE\Microsoft\Cryptography. TPM 2.0's endorsement key and Secure Boot state can also anchor checks. Blizzard has not confirmed which of these Defense Matrix stores, so an exact OW2 identifier list remains unverified.

Will reinstalling Overwatch 2 or Windows remove a hardware ban?

Reinstalling Overwatch 2 replaces game files, not the Battle.net enforcement record or firmware-backed identifiers. Reinstalling Windows can change MachineGuid and a volume serial, but it normally leaves the baseboard serial, motherboard UUID, drive firmware serial, NIC hardware, TPM identity, and Secure Boot state in place. It also cannot reopen the banned Battle.net license. Use an appeal for the account action before diagnosing the device.

Does Overwatch 2 use a kernel-level anti-cheat like Vanguard?

Blizzard does not publish a Defense Matrix architecture document comparable to Riot's Vanguard documentation, so a definite yes or no would overstate the evidence. Its EULA says the running platform may monitor memory for unauthorized programs and may receive system and driver data after crashes. Those permissions establish a broad observation surface, but they do not prove which component runs in kernel mode or which hardware fields Overwatch 2 reads.

Can I appeal an Overwatch 2 cheating or account ban?

Yes. Blizzard Support maintains an Overwatch 2 suspension and ban appeal route, and the registered email should identify the category of action. Submit one clear appeal with the account-security context and any relevant software details. Blizzard may uphold the penalty without disclosing its detection logic. An appeal can review the account action; Blizzard does not publish a separate Overwatch 2 hardware-ban checker or a public process for removing a device fingerprint.

Can XIM, Cronus, or another controller adapter cause an OW2 ban?

Yes, when the device is used to bypass Controller Pool restrictions. Blizzard's August 29, 2025 "Peripheral Vision" update says continued use of unapproved peripherals in that pool can result in a full game-account ban regardless of rank or mode. The policy is about the unfair input advantage, not proof of a PC HWID ban. Native mouse-and-keyboard support or a controller used in its intended pool is a different case.

Can I be banned for grouping with a cheater in Overwatch 2?

Yes. Blizzard announced that players who knowingly and regularly group with accounts flagged for cheating can receive account action even when they are not running the cheat themselves. Its February 1, 2023 Defense Matrix update describes extended suspensions and, in extreme cases, outright bans for that behavior. Random matchmaking with a cheater is not the policy described; the wording targets willing, repeated grouping that benefits from the cheat.

Does an Overwatch 2 ban affect Diablo, WoW, or other Blizzard games?

Do not assume it does. Battle.net is a shared platform, so the same computer and account context can be visible across Blizzard products, creating cross-title correlation exposure. Blizzard has not published a rule saying an Overwatch 2 account closure automatically closes Diablo, World of Warcraft, or every other game license. The banned Overwatch license stays banned, while any separate title action depends on Blizzard's policy and evidence for that title.
